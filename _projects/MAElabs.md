### Summary

During the summer of 2026, I worked with Cornell’s Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering Instructional Labs, primarily supporting the System Dynamics course (MAE 3260). My work focused on improving and expanding the hardware used in several undergraduate laboratory experiments.

For MAE 3260, I helped develop new experimental setups by soldering approximately 200 BNC connectors and designing a custom rack in CAD to organize and house the new connections. I also designed and 3D printed custom adapters for the motor, tachometer, and potentiometer used in a rotating-system experiment, allowing the components to be coupled together through flexible tubing. I soldered a PCB for a vibrations beam experiment and assembled and wired an additional data acquisition setup using an NI USB-6001 and custom BNC connections, creating an extra lab station that had not previously been available.

Beyond MAE 3260, I also assisted with the fabrication of a ramp for a robotics lab. The internship gave me hands-on experience with CAD, 3D printing, soldering, electronics integration, and fabrication while contributing directly to the development and maintenance of instructional laboratory equipment used by Cornell engineering students.
