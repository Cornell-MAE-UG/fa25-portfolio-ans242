Hi, nice to meet you! I’m Alex.

I’m a junior studying Mechanical Engineering at Cornell University. I’ve loved aviation and aerospace since my dad took me flying for the first time at age ten, and I’ve always been fascinated by how engineering can push the boundaries of what’s possible. My dream is to help make the aerospace industry a more susatainable place! I have a lot of hands-on project based and research experience, which has allowed me to build up a strong technical skillset that I'm excited to apply to real-world challenges. 

Beyond engineering, I’m pursuing my private pilot’s license and work as a campus tour guide. Both are roles that have strengthened my communication skills, ability to stay calm under pressure, and confidence working with diverse groups of people. My teammates know me as someone who brings energy, positivity, and collaboration into any environment and I’m excited to carry that into whatever comes next!
