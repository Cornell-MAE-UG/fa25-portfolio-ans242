Hi, nice to meet you! I’m Alex.

I’m a junior studying Mechanical Engineering at Cornell with a lifelong passion for aviation. Ever since my first flight at age ten, I’ve been fascinated by how engineering can push the boundaries of what’s possible. My biggest dream is to someday help make the aerospace and energy industries more sustainable.

I love the full engineering cycle, designing, building, testing, and iterating, and I bring heart, enthusiasm, and a strong sense of teamwork into everything I do.

Outside of engineering, I’m working toward my private pilot’s license and love connecting with people as a campus tour guide.

Feel free to explore my portfolio to see what I’ve been working on!
