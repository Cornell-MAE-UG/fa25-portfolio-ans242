## About Me


![Profile Picture]({{ "/assets/images/profile-pic.jpg" | relative_url }}){: class="profile-image"}

 
My name is {{ site.name }}, and I am I’m Alex, a junior studying Mechanical Engineering at Cornell University. I’ve loved aviation and aerospace since my dad took me flying for the first time at age ten, and I’ve always been fascinated by how engineering can push the boundaries of what’s possible. My dream is to help make the aerospace industry a more susatianable place!
I have a lot of hands-on project based and research experience, which has allowed me to build up a strong technical skillset. I am the propulsion subteam lead of the Cornell University Design Build Fly engineering project team. On my project team, we design and build a new radio controlled aircraft every year based on a ruleset given to us by the AIAA. Leading my subteam has taught me a lot about sizing and optimizing different propulsion systems for different kinds of plane shapes and uses. I’ve gained extensive experience in CAD, designing for manufacturing, machining, thrust testing, and refining motor and battery configurations based on performance data. During my time as lead so far, I’ve overseen the design and construction of a new static thrust testing rig, tow banner release mechanism, and stability control implementation using a gyroscope. 
These experiences have taught me how to analyze problems, iterate quickly, work across subteams, and apply new concepts accurately. I may not have direct industry experience in unmanned systems yet, but I’m a fast and enthusiastic learner who thrives in hands-on environments. This past summer, I helped design, wire, manufacture, and test a prototype ultralight Magnus-effect aircraft, which required adapting to new physics and unfamiliar configurations under tight timelines.
Beyond engineering, I’m pursuing my private pilot’s license and work as a campus tour guide—both roles that have strengthened my communication skills, ability to stay calm under pressure, and confidence working with diverse groups of people. My teammates know me as someone who brings energy, positivity, and collaboration into any environment!
